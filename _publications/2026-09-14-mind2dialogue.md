### Overview

**Mind2Dialogue** generates training conversations using a user simulator and an Oracle assistant that share an evolving simulated mental state. Models learn from the Oracle's responses while receiving only the user's persona and conversation history, without access to the simulated state. Evaluation covers personalization and reasoning about beliefs and actions.
