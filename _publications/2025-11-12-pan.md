### Overview

**PAN** predicts future world states as videos conditioned on past observations and natural-language actions. Its Generative Latent Prediction architecture pairs an autoregressive latent dynamics model with a video diffusion decoder, connecting language-based reasoning with visual simulation. The work evaluates action-conditioned simulation, forecasting over long horizons, and reasoning through simulated futures.
