**Abstract.** CReLeRI presents an explainable, concept-centric framework for video analysis. The system learns interpretable representations and performs multi-step reasoning over concept graphs while supporting human-in-the-loop interaction. CReLeRI delivers transparent predictions with visual and textual rationales, improving trust and debuggability for long-horizon video understanding tasks.
