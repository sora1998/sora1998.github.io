### Overview

This survey examines how time-series analysis is developing into systems that gather evidence, choose tools or actions, receive feedback, and adapt. It organizes the literature around perception, reasoning, planning and action, memory and knowledge, and temporal world models. Reliability and evaluation connect these capabilities, with particular attention to grounding, reproducibility, and decision-making under uncertainty.
