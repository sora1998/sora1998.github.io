**Abstract.** scPilot introduces a reasoning-centric workflow that couples large language models with domain-specific toolchains for end-to-end single-cell analysis. The system automates data ingestion, quality control, normalization, clustering, annotation, and reporting while maintaining traceability and reproducibility. By leveraging iterative tool selection, chain-of-thought planning, and guardrailed execution, scPilot enables rapid hypothesis generation and robust discovery on standard single-cell benchmarks and real-world datasets.
