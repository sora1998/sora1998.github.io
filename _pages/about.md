My name is **Enze Ma**, and I am a Ph.D. student in the [University of Illinois Chicago (UIC)](https://cs.uic.edu/).  
I am fortunate to be advised by Professor [Philip S. Yu](https://cs.uic.edu/profiles/philip-yu/), and I am a member of the **[Big Data and Social Computing (BDSC) Lab](https://bdsc.lab.uic.edu/)**.

My current research interests lie at the intersection of **Large Language Models (LLMs)**, **AI Agents**, and **Multimodality**.

Prior to joining UIC, I was a research internship at the [University of California San Diego (UCSD)](https://cse.ucsd.edu/) with [Professor Zhiting Hu](https://zhiting.ucsd.edu/).  
I received both my **M.S.** and **B.S.** in Computer Science from [UC San Diego](https://cse.ucsd.edu/).
